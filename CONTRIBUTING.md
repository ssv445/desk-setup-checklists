# Contributing

Corrections are welcome. A list is only useful if every line is right.

1. **Send a correction as a pull request.** Change the line in the list's file.
2. **Add a source.** Link a public guide from a body such as CCOHS, OSHA, a university ergonomics group or a standards body. A line without a source won't be merged.
3. **Keep the three parts.** Measure at home, check in the shop, walk away if. A new check goes in the part where you'd do it.
4. **Keep it testable.** Each check is a measurement, or a test you can do sitting down in about 30 seconds.
5. **Keep it plain.** Short sentences, everyday words, centimetres with inches in brackets.

Please don't add:

1. Brand or model names, or affiliate links.
2. Medical claims, or advice to treat pain.

For anything else, open an issue.
