# Desk setup checklist: what to check before you buy

You stand in the shop, sit in a chair for ten seconds, and it feels fine. Two weeks later your feet dangle and the armrests keep you away from the desk.

The short answer: measure your height once. It gives your chair seat, desk and screen heights. Then take the checklist for each item to the shop, tick each line, and walk away if you see anything on its "walk away" list.

**Your numbers:** your seat, desk and screen heights come from your height. For numbers worked out from your height, open the [home office setup checklist](https://poxture.com/guides/desk-setup-checklist) and enter it once.

## Measure once, at home

1. **Your height, barefoot.** Stand against a wall, mark the top of your head, and measure from the floor to the mark. [1]
   - If you work in shoes, note that too. Every height goes up a little.
2. **Your desk's height, if you keep your desk.** Measure from the floor to the top of the desk. Your new chair has to fit it.
3. **Your floor.** Carpet or a hard floor. It decides the chair wheels you need. [3]

## The checklists

1. **Chair.** [Office chair checklist](office-chair.md): seat height, seat depth, lower-back support, armrests and wheels.
2. **Desk.** [Desk checklist](desk.md): height, depth and leg room, sitting or standing.
3. **Monitor.** [Monitor checklist](monitor.md): size for your desk, and a stand that reaches your eye level.

Later, on the same three-part list: a monitor arm or laptop stand, a keyboard, a mouse, a mouse pad, and a footrest (a box or a step stool).

## How to use a checklist

Every list has the same three parts:

1. **Measure at home before you go.** A few numbers, written down once.
2. **Check in the shop or on the product page.** Each line is a measurement, or a test you can do sitting down in about 30 seconds.
3. **Walk away if you see this.** One failed line here is enough to say no.
